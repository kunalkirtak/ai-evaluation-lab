Machine learning is a field of artificial intelligence in which systems learn patterns from data rather than following explicitly programmed rules. A model is trained on historical examples, adjusts its internal parameters to minimize prediction error, and is then used to make predictions on new, unseen data. Common categories include supervised learning, where labeled examples guide training, unsupervised learning, where the model finds structure in unlabeled data, and reinforcement learning, where an agent learns by receiving rewards or penalties for its actions.
